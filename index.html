Gardening Knowledge

Essential Plant Care Guide

Simple expert tips to help your green leafy friends thrive and stay vibrant year-round.

Watering Wisdom

Check soil moisture before watering. Overwatering causes root rot. Always ensure proper pot drainage holes.

Sunlight Needs

Match plant variety with appropriate light. Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect sunlight daily.

Soil & Fertilizer

Use well-draining organic potting mix. Feed with vermicompost every 3-4 weeks during active growth.

Pest Protection

Inspect leaves regularly. Wipe dust off leaves and use organic Neem oil spray to deter insects safely.
